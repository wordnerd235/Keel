The plan

  1. Eat about – calories a day. That is roughly 450 under maintenance, for ¾ lb a week. Track with a free app (Cronometer or MyFitnessPal) for the first month to learn portions; after that, tracking dinner loosely is often enough.
  2. Hit – g of protein daily. This is the biggest lever for keeping muscle while you lose fat, and it keeps you full. Aim for 35 to 40 g at each meal.
  3. Lift at home 3 times a week, about 35 minutes. Two 20 lb dumbbells, alternating A and B (Mon A, Wed B, Fri A, then flip). 3 sets each, 60–90 seconds rest, stop each set 1–2 reps before you can't do another. This matters more than cardio.

  4. Walk about – steps a day. Cheaper and easier to recover from than extra cardio sessions.
  5. Sleep 7+ hours. Short sleep makes hunger worse and costs muscle on a diet.
  6. Adjust only from the trend. Check the 7‑day average once a week. If it drops less than ½ lb two weeks in a row, cut 150 calories. If it drops more than 1½ lb a week, add 150. Ignore single days.

Family dinners that fit

  • Chicken taco bowls or burrito bowls with rice, salsa, cheese, and lettuce (meat mixed in, kids build their own).
  • Turkey or lean beef spaghetti with meat sauce; you take a smaller pasta portion and more sauce.
  • Chicken fried rice with frozen veggies, made without egg.
  • Sheet-pan chicken fajitas; Greek yogurt in place of sour cream.
  • Snacks: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, string cheese, beef jerky, protein shakes.
